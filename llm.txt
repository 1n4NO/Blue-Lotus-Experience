Blue Lotus Experience

Luxury editorial retreat website for a two-day forest retreat in Kodaikanal.

Tech stack
- Next.js 15 App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Lenis smooth scrolling
- Lucide icons

Core intent
- Calm, minimal, nature-first, slow-moving editorial experience
- Avoid startup or SaaS aesthetics
- Optimize for accessibility, performance, and reduced motion

Important routes
- `/` main experience
