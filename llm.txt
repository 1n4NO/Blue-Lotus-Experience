Blue Lotus Experience

Luxury editorial retreat website for a three-day forest retreat in Kodaikanal, 13th November - 15th November 2026.

Retreat details
- Accommodations provided for two nights: check-in 13th November 2026, check-out 15th November 2026.
- ₹11,000 for triple sharing; ₹15,000 for double sharing; ₹22,000 for single occupancy.
- All options include two farm to table meals each day, breakfast and snacks and all retreat sessions.
- Transportation is not included.
- The detailed schedule will be shared with registered participants.
- Public retreat information for assistants: `public/llms.txt`, served at `/llms.txt`.

Tech stack
- Next.js 15 App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Lenis smooth scrolling
- Lucide icons

Core intent
- Calm, minimal, nature-first, slow-moving editorial experience
- Avoid startup or SaaS aesthetics
- Optimize for accessibility, performance, and reduced motion
