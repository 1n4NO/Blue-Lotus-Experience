# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring the Blue Lotus Experience retreat, including visitors arriving from Instagram and Facebook ads.

## Product Purpose

Introduce the Kodaikanal retreat and capture enquiries from interested participants.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The /apply landing page asks only for Name, Email, Phone, and Location. Details should be captured as fields are filled. Submit confirms the enquiry and reveals a link to the main website. Captured details are delivered to the team using Resend, matching the existing application email flow.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the current Blue Lotus Experience website's look and feel, language, and existing assets.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed retreat facts are in content/site.ts and public/llms.txt. Retreat dates are 13–15 November 2026, with check-in 13 November and check-out 16 November. Accommodations cost ₹15,000 double sharing or ₹22,000 single occupancy, including three nights, two meals daily, and all sessions.
