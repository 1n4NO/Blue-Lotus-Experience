# Blue Lotus Experience
# Email Communication Guide

> Every email is an extension of the retreat.
>
> It should feel like receiving a handwritten letter from someone who has been waiting for your arrival—not a marketing campaign.

---

# Email Philosophy

Blue Lotus emails are written with the same restraint as the retreat itself.

They should never feel automated.

They should never feel sales-driven.

They should never overwhelm.

Every message should leave a little space.

---

# Tone of Voice

- Warm
- Personal
- Quiet
- Thoughtful
- Slow
- Honest
- Human

Avoid:

- Marketing language
- Urgency
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Emoji
- Corporate wording

Every email should read like a letter.

---

# Email Structure

Every email follows a simple rhythm.

```
Greeting

One thoughtful introduction.

Main message.

Gentle closing.

Signature.
```

Example:

> Hello Pratik,
>
> Thank you for writing to us.
>
> Your application has safely arrived.
>
> We'll read it carefully over the coming days.
>
> Until then,
>
> We hope you find a little stillness today.
>
> —
> Blue Lotus Experience

# WAITLIST EMAILS

## 01 — The First Letter

**Trigger**
Immediately after someone joins the waitlist.

### Purpose

Welcome them.

Introduce the philosophy.

Set expectations.

### Subject Ideas

- Welcome to Blue Lotus
- The First Letter
- Thank you for finding us

---

## 02 — Why We Created Blue Lotus

Explain the story.

Talk about:

- slowing down
- presence
- forests
- tea
- silence

No selling.

---

## 03 — Why Only Eleven?

Explain why intimacy matters.

Quality over scale.

Conversation over crowds.

---

## 04 — What Two Days Can Feel Like

Don't list a schedule.

Instead describe moments.

- Morning mist
- Tea
- Campfire
- Journaling
- Rain

---

## 05 — Meet Kodaikanal

Introduce the forest.

The weather.

The rhythm.

---

## 06 — Applications Open

A gentle invitation.

Not an announcement.

---

# APPLICATION EMAILS

## 07 — Your Letter Has Arrived

Trigger:

Application submitted.

### Purpose

Confirm receipt.

Explain timeline.

Reassure them that every application is read personally.

---

## 08 — We Have A Few Questions

Optional.

Request clarification if needed.

---

## 09 — A Place Has Been Kept For You

Acceptance email.

The most important email.

Do not say:

> Congratulations!

Instead:

> We'd love to welcome you to Blue Lotus.

Include:

- acceptance
- next steps
- payment link
- deadline

---

## 10 — You're On Our Waiting List

Warm.

Hopeful.

Respectful.

---

## 11 — Until Our Paths Cross Again

Decline email.

Thank them sincerely.

Invite them to future retreats.

---

# PAYMENT EMAILS

## 12 — The Path Is Open

Payment instructions.

Minimal.

Beautiful.

Clear.

---

## 13 — We Have Received Your Payment

Confirmation.

Gratitude.

Warmth.

---

## 14 — Gentle Reminder

Only if necessary.

Respectful.

Never pushy.

---

# BEFORE ARRIVAL

These emails build anticipation.

---

## 15 — Welcome

Immediately after payment.

Introduce the journey ahead.

---

## 16 — Before You Leave Home

Sent approximately two weeks before.

Include:

- what to bring
- comfortable clothes
- journal
- walking shoes
- water bottle

Remind people not to overpack.

---

## 17 — One Week To Go

Travel guidance.

Accommodation suggestions.

Weather.

Directions.

Arrival time.

---

## 18 — A Few Days Before

Three days before arrival.

Very short.

Encourage rest.

---

## 19 — Tomorrow, We Meet

Night before.

Keep it simple.

Example:

> Sleep well.
>
> Tomorrow,
>
> we'll meet beneath the trees.

---

## 20 — Good Morning

Sent on retreat morning.

Meeting point.

Arrival reminder.

Warm welcome.

---

# DURING THE RETREAT

Usually unnecessary.

Only send if needed.

Examples:

- Weather update
- Meeting location
- Schedule adjustment

---

# AFTER THE RETREAT

This is where relationships begin.

---

## 21 — Thank You

Sent the evening after.

Express gratitude.

No requests.

---

## 22 — The Forest Stays With You

One week later.

Reflection prompts.

Journaling questions.

Encourage continued practice.

---

## 23 — Your Photographs

Share the gallery.

Invite them to revisit the experience.

---

## 24 — We'd Love To Hear From You

Feedback request.

Simple.

Personal.

---

## 25 — Stay Close

Invite them to:

- future retreats
- newsletter
- community

---

## 26 — A Place Before Anyone Else

Priority invitation for returning participants.

---

# Writing Guidelines

Every email should:

- Leave space.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Avoid pressure.
- Feel handwritten.
- Respect the reader's attention.

Never sound like marketing.
