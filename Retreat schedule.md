# Blue Lotus Experience
## Retreat Schedule

> *Your body is your temple. Nature is your medicine.*

---

# Two Days in the Forest

This is not an itinerary to complete.

It is an invitation to slow down.

Each experience has been intentionally designed to create space—for rest, reflection, conversation, and reconnection with nature.

Times are approximate. Presence matters more than the clock.

---

# Day One

## 9:00 AM — Arrival

Participants arrive at the retreat venue.

Welcome tea is served while everyone settles into the space.

Phones are encouraged to remain on silent.

---

## 9:45 AM — Opening Circle

A gentle introduction.

We'll meet one another, share intentions, and begin creating a space built on trust, curiosity, and presence.

---

## 10:30 AM — Forest Walk

A slow guided walk through the surrounding rainforest.

There is no destination.

Only observation.

Participants are encouraged to walk mostly in silence, noticing sounds, textures, light, and movement.

---

## 12:30 PM — Farm-to-Table Lunch

A seasonal vegetarian meal prepared using locally sourced ingredients.

The meal is enjoyed without hurry.

Conversation is welcome.

Silence is equally welcome.

---

## 2:00 PM — Rest & Reflection

Unstructured time.

Journal.

Read.

Nap.

Sit beneath a tree.

Watch the rain.

Do nothing.

---

## 3:30 PM — Tea Ceremony

A slow tea ritual inspired by mindfulness rather than tradition.

This is an opportunity to practice attention through simple actions.

---

## 4:30 PM — Guided Journaling

Prompt-based reflective writing.

No writing experience is required.

The goal is honesty, not perfection.

Suggested themes include:

- What have I been carrying?
- What deserves my attention?
- What am I ready to let go of?

---

## 6:00 PM — Sunset Walk

A quiet walk through the forest as evening approaches.

Observe how the landscape changes with fading light.

---

## 7:00 PM — Campfire Dinner & Conversation

Dinner is shared around a fire.

The evening is intentionally unstructured.

Stories emerge naturally.

No workshops.

No presentations.

Just conversation.

---

## 9:00 PM — Closing Silence

Before leaving for the evening, participants spend a few quiet minutes together.

No instructions.

Just stillness.

---

# Day Two

## 7:00 AM — Sunrise Gathering

Meet outdoors.

Watch the forest wake.

No agenda.

Simply notice.

---

## 7:45 AM — Breath & Presence

Gentle breathing exercises suitable for everyone.

No advanced practice.

No performance.

Only awareness.

---

## 8:30 AM — Tea & Light Breakfast

Fresh fruit.

Seasonal local food.

Handcrafted tea.

Slow conversation.

---

## 9:30 AM — Solo Reflection Walk

Participants explore individually.

Optional journaling prompts are provided.

Walk slowly.

Pause often.

Leave no trace.

---

## 11:00 AM — Closing Circle

We gather once more.

Participants are invited—but never required—to share what they are taking home from the experience.

---

## 12:00 PM — Farewell Lunch

A final shared meal.

Simple.

Seasonal.

Nourishing.

---

## 1:30 PM — Departure

No formal goodbye.

Only gratitude.

The retreat ends.

The practice continues.

# Our Principles

We value:

- Presence over productivity.
- Curiosity over certainty.
- Listening over speaking.
- Simplicity over excess.
- Nature over distraction.

---

# A Gentle Reminder

There is nothing to achieve here.

Nothing to prove.

Nothing to optimize.

For two days, allow yourself to move at the pace of the forest.
